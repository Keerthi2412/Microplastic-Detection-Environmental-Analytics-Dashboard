**Problem Statement:** 
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Microplastics have become a significant environmental and public health concern. They contaminate drinking water and may enter the human body through the food chain, potentially causing inflammation, toxic chemical exposure, and organ damage. Existing detection systems are expensive and hardware-intensive, making them inaccessible for small organizations and communities. There is a need for a software platform that can analyze laboratory reports, historical datasets, and water quality records to identify contamination trends, predict high-risk locations, and assist decision-makers.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Solution Overview:** 
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Microplastic pollution in drinking water is becoming a serious environmental problem because of improper plastic waste disposal. Water quality data is often stored in different databases, making it difficult to access, combine, and analyze. Reviewing water quality reports manually also takes a lot of time and effort. There is no single system to bring all this information together and provide useful insights. Therefore, an **analytical dashboard** is needed to collect and organize water quality data, visualize it through charts and graphs, identify pollution patterns, and help understand the possible causes of water quality problems.
