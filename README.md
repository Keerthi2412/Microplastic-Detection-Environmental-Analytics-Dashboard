**Project Title:** Microplastic-Detection-Environmental-Analytics-Dashboard
**Team Name:** InsightX
**Problem Statement:** Microplastics have become a significant environmental and public health concern. They contaminate drinking water and may enter the human body through the food chain, potentially causing inflammation, toxic chemical exposure, and organ damage. Existing detection systems are expensive and hardware-intensive, making them inaccessible for small organizations and communities.

There is a need for a software platform that can analyze laboratory reports, historical datasets, and water quality records to identify contamination trends, predict high-risk locations, and assist decision-makers.
